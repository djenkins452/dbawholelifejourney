1 · One canvas, three visible methods

The blank page is the default and is immediately usable. The three methods live on one bar — clearly labeled, never a forced gateway, never hidden.

2 · Talk It Through — the voice session

Full conversational voice with a Chief of Staff that knows the user. Both sides transcribed live. The entry assembles behind the scenes and is revealed at the end.

3 · Journal reveal & authorship review

The delight moment: a finished entry, in the user's voice, assembled at the end. Review is authorship of your own words — not reviewing a machine's guess.

4 · Write Together

The user writes, then invites the CoS. It reads the current draft and asks one useful question at a time. Answer by voice or text; return to writing alone anytime. No unsolicited interruptions.

5 · Recovery & failure states

The requirement: the user never loses a word. Every failure degrades to "you can still write," with the draft intact. No state is expressed in jargon.

6 · Desktop

Same one-canvas IA and three visible methods. The page breathes; voice shows the transcript in a calm side column. The model does not fork — layout adapts.

7 · Truth Discovery & Publishing

The user only journals. The conversation is the source; the Journal is the guaranteed publication. If the CoS recognizes a truth that deserves to live beyond today, it offers one quiet candidate — after the entry is saved, never during. WLJ (not the model) creates the deterministic object, links it to the source, and audits it. Existing domains only. Most sessions produce no suggestion at all.
