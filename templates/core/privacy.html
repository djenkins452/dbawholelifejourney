Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Our Commitment

At Whole Life Journey, we believe your personal reflections, health data, and spiritual journey belong to you. We're committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (for authentication and communication)
  • Name (optional, for personalization)
  • Date of birth (required, for age verification - see Children's Privacy section)
  • Password (stored securely using industry-standard hashing)
  • Avatar image (optional)

Journal & Reflection Data

Content you create for personal journaling:

  • Journal entries (title, body text, entry date)
  • Mood selections (great, good, okay, low, difficult)
  • Emotion tags (multiple selections from predefined emotions)
  • Custom tags and categories you create
  • Responses to journal prompts

Health & Wellness Data

Health metrics you track manually or sync from devices:

  • Body composition: Weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass
  • Heart health: Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood pressure, blood oxygen
  • Activity: Steps, active/resting calories, distance, flights climbed, exercise minutes, stand hours, workouts, VO2 max
  • Sleep: Sleep duration, quality, stage breakdown, respiratory rate
  • Nutrition: Food entries, calories, macronutrients, water intake, caffeine, fasting windows
  • Blood glucose: Glucose readings (manual or from Dexcom integration)
  • Medications: Intake logs, refill tracking, dosage information
  • Female health: Menstrual cycle data including flow level, symptoms, mood, and fertility predictions (if you enable this feature)
  • Body temperature: Temperature readings

Faith & Spiritual Data

If you use the faith features:

  • Prayer requests (title, description, who you're praying for, priority, answered status)
  • Scripture verses you save or bookmark
  • Bible reading plan progress and preferences
  • Faith milestones and reflections
  • Personal notes on Bible passages

Life Planning Data

If you use life planning features:

  • Goals and progress tracking
  • Task lists and reminders
  • Life areas and priorities you define

Technical Information

Automatically collected when you use the Service:

  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Browser type and device information
  • Usage patterns (pages visited, features used)
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Mobile App Data

If you use our iOS mobile app:

  • Device information: Device model, OS version, app version, device name
  • Authentication tokens: Secure API tokens for mobile access (encrypted)
  • Push notifications: Device push token (if you enable notifications)
  • HealthKit data: Health metrics synced from Apple Health (only with your explicit permission - see below)

Apple HealthKit Integration

If you use our iOS app and grant HealthKit permissions, we can sync health data from Apple Health. This requires your explicit authorization for each data type:

  • Steps, distance, flights climbed, exercise minutes
  • Active and resting calories burned
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood pressure, blood oxygen
  • Weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass
  • Sleep analysis and respiratory rate
  • Blood glucose readings
  • Water and caffeine intake
  • Mindful minutes and workouts
  • Body temperature

Important: HealthKit data is never used for advertising and is stored securely. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in your iPhone's Settings app under Privacy & Security > Health > Whole Life Journey.

Health Information Disclaimer

Whole Life Journey provides informational insights only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Security & Authentication Data

To protect your account, we may collect:

  • Biometric credentials: If you enable passkey/WebAuthn login, we store your public key credential (not your biometric data itself)
  • Multi-factor authentication: Verification codes and their status
  • Fraud prevention: Device fingerprint hashes, risk scores, CAPTCHA verification results
  • Login history: IP addresses and timestamps of authentication attempts

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Display your content back to you
  • Generate AI-powered insights (only if you enable this feature)
  • Send important service notifications
  • Improve and develop the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Use your content for advertising purposes
  • Share your journal entries with anyone
  • Train AI models on your personal content without explicit consent

AI Features and Your Data

Whole Life Journey offers AI-powered features to help you reflect, plan, and track your wellness journey. If you enable AI features, here's how your data is used:

What AI Features Collect

  • Conversation history: Your messages and the AI's responses are stored to provide continuity
  • AI profile: Optional personal context you provide (age, family, interests, goals, health conditions) to personalize responses
  • Learned facts: Information the AI learns about you during conversations (stored encrypted)
  • Coaching preferences: Your selected AI coaching style
  • Generated insights: Daily, weekly, and monthly summaries created from your data
  • Usage logs: API calls, token counts, and costs (for service operation)

How AI Processing Works

  • AI features require your explicit consent before activation
  • Your data is sent to OpenAI's API to generate responses and insights
  • We do not allow OpenAI to use your data for training their models
  • Conversations are associated with your account but not shared with other users
  • You can view, export, or delete your AI conversation history

AI Consent Levels

  • Basic AI consent: Enables AI insights and daily check-ins
  • Personal Assistant consent: Allows deeper data access for more personalized coaching

You can adjust or revoke AI consent at any time in your settings. When disabled, your content is not processed by AI systems, though existing conversation history is retained until you delete it.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard protections:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls limiting who can access systems

While we implement strong security measures, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Active Data: We retain your content for as long as your account is active and you wish to keep it.

Archived Data: Archived entries are retained until you choose to delete them or delete your account.

Deleted Data: When you delete content, it enters a 30-day grace period during which you can restore it. After 30 days, it is permanently deleted from our systems.

Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Some data may be retained longer if required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have about you
  • Correct: Update or correct your information
  • Delete: Delete your content or entire account
  • Export: Download a copy of your data
  • Restrict: Limit how we process your data
  • Object: Opt out of certain data uses

To exercise these rights, visit your account settings or contact us at admin@wholelifejourney.com.

Google Calendar Integration

If you choose to connect your Google Calendar, we request access to your calendar data through Google's OAuth system. This integration:

  • Requires your explicit consent before connecting
  • Uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope to read and write calendar events
  • Stores OAuth tokens securely and encrypted in our database
  • Only accesses the calendar(s) you explicitly select for syncing

What We Access

  • Calendar event details (title, date, time, location, description)
  • List of your calendars (to let you choose which to sync)

How We Use Calendar Data

  • Two-way sync: Events you create in Whole Life Journey can be synced to Google Calendar
  • Import: Events from Google Calendar can be imported into Whole Life Journey
  • Calendar data is only used to provide the sync feature you requested
  • We do not share your calendar data with any third parties
  • We do not use your calendar data for advertising or marketing

Disconnecting Google Calendar

You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time from your calendar settings. When you disconnect:

  • We immediately revoke our access to your Google Calendar
  • Stored OAuth credentials are deleted from our database
  • Previously synced events remain in Whole Life Journey (you can delete them manually)

You can also revoke access directly from your Google Account permissions.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13.

Age Verification

We require all users to provide their date of birth during account registration. If a user indicates they are under 13 years old, we will:

  • Prevent account creation
  • Not collect or store any personal information
  • Display an age-appropriate message explaining the requirement

Discovery of Underage Users

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will:

  • Immediately terminate the account
  • Delete all personal information associated with that account
  • Take reasonable steps to prevent future unauthorized access

Parental Rights

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created an account or provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at admin@wholelifejourney.com. You have the right to:

  • Request information about data collected from your child
  • Request deletion of your child's data
  • Refuse further collection of your child's information

Users Aged 13-17

Users between 13 and 17 years of age may use the Service, but we recommend they do so with the awareness and consent of a parent or guardian. Parents are encouraged to:

  • Review the Service and its features with their teen
  • Discuss appropriate use of personal journaling and health tracking
  • Monitor their teen's use of the Service as appropriate

International Users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you are accessing from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

Data Transfers
