# Bible Reading Plans Roadmap

**Created:** 2026-01-20
**Status:** In Progress
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-20 (Ruth deployed)

This document tracks the development of Bible reading plans for the Whole Life Journey app. Each plan follows the established structure with three difficulty levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and includes context summaries, commentary at each level, and reflection prompts.

---

## Quality Standards

All reading plans MUST meet these standards before deployment:

### Biblical Accuracy
- All Scripture references must be verified
- Historical context must be accurate and supported by scholarship
- No denominational bias - non-denominational, Bible-based content only
- No assumptions or speculation presented as fact
- Cross-references must be accurate

### Difficulty Level Appropriateness
- **Beginner**: Simple language, foundational concepts, practical application focus, minimal technical terms
- **Intermediate**: Deeper context, cross-references, theological connections, some Greek/Hebrew terms with explanations
- **Advanced**: Scholarly depth, original language insights, intertextual connections, theological nuances, historical-critical context

### Readability
- Clear, engaging writing
- Appropriate length for each level (Beginner shorter, Advanced can be longer)
- Reflection prompts that are personal and actionable
