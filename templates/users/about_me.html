How this works

Whole Life Journey remembers. The AI provider processes.

What you keep here is stored in your Whole Life Journey account — that’s why your Chief of Staff still knows it tomorrow. When it needs to answer you, the relevant parts are sent to our AI provider to generate the reply. The provider isn’t where your Chief of Staff’s memory lives — this page is.
