You’re in charge of the conversation

Just say so, in your own words:

  • “skip” or “move on” — leave that one
  • “tell me more” or “go deeper” — keep going on this
  • “just the basics” — keep it light
  • “that’s enough about family” — done with that subject
  • “let’s come back to this later” — park it for now
  • “I’d rather not discuss that” — I won’t bring it up again
  • “don’t remember that” — I’ll leave it out
  • “stop for now” — we’ll pick up later
