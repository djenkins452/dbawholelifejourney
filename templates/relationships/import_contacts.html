How to export contacts from your phone

iPhone

  1. Open the Contacts app
  2. Tap Lists (top left) and select All Contacts
  3. Tap the ⋯ menu (top right) → Select All
  4. Tap Share → choose Share as vCard
  5. Send it to yourself via AirDrop, email, or Messages
  6. Open the .vcf file on this device and upload it here

Android

  1. Open the Contacts app
  2. Tap Fix & manage (bottom) → Export to file
  3. Choose the account to export and tap Export
  4. The .vcf file will be saved to your Downloads folder
  5. Transfer the file to this device and upload it here

Google Contacts

  1. Go to contacts.google.com
  2. Select the contacts you want to export (or select all)
  3. Click Export → choose vCard format
  4. Upload the downloaded .vcf file here
